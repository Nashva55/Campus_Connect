Practice Areas

What to prepare before a mock interview

Strong interviews come from repetition, not just reading questions once.

Area 1

Self introduction

Prepare a short, confident introduction that covers your background, strengths, and current focus.

Area 2

Project explanation

Be ready to explain what you built, why you built it, and what problems you solved.

Area 3

Technical basics

Review concepts from your target domain so you can explain them simply without panic.

Practice Routine

A simple mock interview cycle

Use this structure with a friend, mentor, or by recording yourself.

Round 1

Warm-up questions

  • Tell me about yourself.
  • What domain are you targeting and why?
  • What is one project you are proud of?

Round 2

Technical explanation

  • Explain one concept from your domain in simple terms.
  • Walk through a project architecture or workflow.
  • Answer one debugging or problem-solving question.

Round 3

Feedback and improvement

  • Note unclear answers and repeated filler words.
  • Improve confidence, pacing, and structure.
  • Repeat the same round after small corrections.
