Structure

What your student resume should include

Keep it concise, relevant, and honest. One strong page is usually better than a long unfocused document.

Section 1

Header and contact

Add your name, phone, email, LinkedIn, GitHub, and portfolio if available.

Section 2

Education

Include college, degree, expected graduation year, and strong academic highlights if they help.

Section 3

Skills and projects

List real technical skills and back them up with clear projects and outcomes.

Writing Flow

How to improve it step by step

Work through this sequence instead of trying to perfect everything at once.

Step 1

Collect your raw content

  • List your skills, certifications, projects, and achievements.
  • Gather links to GitHub, LinkedIn, and live demos.
  • Choose only the content relevant to the role you want.

Step 2

Write project bullets clearly

  • State what you built.
  • Mention the tools you used.
  • Show the outcome, users, or problem solved.

Step 3

Tailor it to the role

  • Frontend roles should highlight UI projects and JavaScript tools.
  • Data roles should highlight Python, SQL, and dataset work.
  • Cloud and security roles should surface labs, certifications, and deployment work.
